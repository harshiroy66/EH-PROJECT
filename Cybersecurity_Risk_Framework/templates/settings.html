Exact Risk Scoring Formula Specification
Academic Baseline

The automated risk engine executes the following standardized weighted linear regression equation to compute the overall risk score:

R = 0.40(V) + 0.25(A) + 0.20(E) + 0.15(S)
Variable Factor Description Weight Permitted Range
V Vulnerability Severity 40% (0.40) 0 – 100
A Asset Criticality 25% (0.25) 0 – 100
E Exposure Level 20% (0.20) 0 – 100
S Security Event Severity 15% (0.15) 0 – 100
Risk Classification Tiers
0 – 100 Scale
Score Range Classification Posture Definition Required Response Action
0 – 24 Low Risk Negligible or contained vulnerability Routine security monitoring & updates
25 – 49 Medium Risk Moderate operational threat profile Apply patches & review permissions
50 – 74 High Risk Elevated exposure with potential breach Prioritize remediation & review network auth
75 – 100 Critical Risk Severe active or easily exploitable flaw Escalate to admin, immediate remediation & isolation
